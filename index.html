Type Math

A 17 font at 150% line height is a 25.5 line, which is 3.19 units of an 8 grid, not a whole number of units.

Boundary: arithmetic only Type Math works out type sizes, line heights and line lengths from the numbers you type, in one unit of your choice. It does not know any screen, printer, font or reader, and it gives no verdict on whether a size, a line height or a line length is readable or good. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: base 16 at ratio 3/2, a 17 font at 150% on an 8 unit, and a 640 column at 8.4 per character.

The scale truth

Sizes from 2 steps down to 5 steps up at ratio 3/2 on a base of 16: 7.11, 10.67, 16, 24, 36, 54, 81, 121.5.

The rhythm truth

A 17 font at 150% line height has a 25.5 line on a baseline unit of 8: that is 3.19 units. The next whole-unit line height is 32, which is 188.24% of the font size.

The measure truth

A column 640 wide at 8.4 per character holds 76 characters per line. A text of 1200 characters takes 16 lines, and the last line holds 60.
