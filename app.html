Type Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out type sizes, line heights and line lengths from the numbers you type, in one unit of your choice. It does not know any screen, printer, font or reader, and it gives no verdict on whether a size, a line height or a line length is readable or good. Every number is your own entry.

The scale truth

Each size is the one before it times the ratio. Give the ratio as two whole numbers with a slash, like 3/2 or 5/4.

The rhythm truth

A line height as a percent of the font size, set against a baseline unit.

The measure truth

How many characters fit on a line of the column, and how many lines a text takes. The character width is the average you measured, up to 2 decimals.

Type Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
